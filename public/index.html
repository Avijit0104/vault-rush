How to Play

Every round, cash boxes parachute into the vault. You have 30 seconds to tap one thing:

📦
Tap a box to CLAIM it.
If several players pick the same box, the cash is split between them. The biggest box isn't always the best pick!
⚔️
Tap a rival to RAID them.
Steal 50% of their unsecured cash (shared if others raid them too). If they lock their vault, you're blocked: you lose a ❤️ and half your unsecured cash goes to them.
🔒
Tap your vault to SECURE.
Moves your unsecured cash into your vault (5% bank fee). Vault cash can never be stolen, and you block every raid that round.
👑
Bounty: the richest player wears the crown. Raiding them steals 60%, so anyone can catch up!
🎲
Random events: 🌟 Golden Box (×2), 🚨 High Alert (blocked raiders lose everything), 🌑 Blackout (values hidden), 🎁 Bonus Drop (extra box), 🏦 Bank Holiday (no fee).
❤️
Everyone starts with 4 lives. At 0 you're eliminated and become a spectator.
🔥
Endgame: the last 3 rounds pay ×1.5, ×2 and ×3. In the FINAL HEIST vaults are jammed, so nobody can secure!
🏆
Most total cash (vault + unsecured) at the end wins. Ties go to whoever has more lives.

Tip: long-press any player to see their habits: how often they secure and raid.
