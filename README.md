## 🎮 Ways to play

- **Play vs Bots:** jump straight in against 3–5 bots with different personalities: greedy, cautious, aggressive and sneaky.
- **Create Room:** get a 4-letter room code. Share it with friends as a link, a code or a QR code. Empty seats fill with bots.
- **Join Room:** enter your friend's code or open the link they sent you. If the game has already started, you watch and get a seat in the next game.

## 🕹 How to play

Every round, cash boxes parachute into the middle of the vault. You have **15, 20 or 30 seconds** (the host chooses) to tap **one** thing:

| Tap | What happens |
|---|---|
| 📦 **A box** | **Claim** its cash. If other players pick the same box, the cash is **split** between you, so the biggest box isn't always the best choice! |
| ⚔️ **A rival** | **Raid** them and steal **50%** of the unsecured cash in their crate. But if they secured that round, you're **blocked**: you lose a ❤️ and half your unsecured cash goes to them. |
| 🔒 **Your safe** | **Secure** your crate's cash into your bank (5% fee). Banked cash can never be stolen, and you block every raid on you that round. |

- Everyone chooses **secretly**. When the timer hits 0, all moves happen at once.
- Changed your mind? Tap something else before time runs out. Tap your choice again to cancel it.
- Done early? Press **LOCK IN** to speed things up.

### 🧺 Crate and 🏦 bank
Each player has a **crate** (unsecured cash, which fills up with coins) and a **bank safe** (secured cash).
A crate that's overflowing starts to shake and glow red. That player is a juicy target!

### ❤️ Lives
Everyone starts with **4 lives**. You only lose one when your raid gets blocked. At 0 lives you're **busted** and watch the rest of the game.

### 👑 Bounty
The richest player wears the crown. Raiding them steals **60%** instead of 50%, so anyone can catch up.

### 🎲 Random events
| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| 🌟 Golden Box | One box is worth **double** |
| 🚨 High Alert | Blocked raiders lose **all** their unsecured cash |
| 🌑 Blackout | Box values stay hidden until time runs out |
| 🎁 Bonus Drop | An extra box drops |
| 🏦 Bank Holiday | No fee for securing |

### 🔥 Endgame
The last three rounds get more dangerous: **Heat Round ×1.5**, **Danger Round ×2**, and the **Final Heist ×3**, where **all safes are jammed**, so nobody can secure!

### 🏆 Winning
The player with the most **total cash (bank + crate)** at the end wins. Ties go to whoever has more lives. Awards like *Master Thief*, *Vault Keeper* and *Iron Wall* go to the standout players.
